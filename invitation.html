Education Champions Gala Dinner 2026

28th November 2026

Dear Ms Iddah Akoth Ogutu

Hope for Kenya Slum Adolescents Initiative cordially invites you to the Education Champions Gala Dinner 2026. Join us to an inspiring evening dedicated to celebrating impact, honoring champions of change, and mobilizing resources to expand educational and empowerment opportunities for vulnerable adolescents and young people living in informal settlements across Kenya.

Under the theme "Delivering Hope Through Education and Empowerment", this special event will bring together partners, supporters, community leaders, corporate organizations, and friends of HKSAI to reflect on achievements, strengthen partnerships, and mobilize resources to invest in the future of the next generation.

📅 Date: Saturday, 28th November 2026
🕕 Time: 5:00 PM – 10:00 PM
📍 Venue: Heron Hotel, Nairobi
🎯 Theme: Delivering Hope Through Education and Empowerment

Evening Highlights

  • Reception and Networking
  • Gourmet dinner experience
  • HKSAI impact showcase and beneficiary stories
  • Education Champion donation and fundraising session
  • Keynote address by a distinguished guest speaker
  • Live entertainment and special performances
  • Recognition of partners, sponsors, and donors
  • Photography
